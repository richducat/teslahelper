About TeslaHelper

TeslaHelper is a small, owner-led project that turns daily Tesla ownership questions into actionable guides. We focus on clear steps, real-world checklists, and accessory recommendations that are easy to verify.

What we do

  • Create step-by-step guides for charging, delivery day, and seasonal ownership.
  • Curate accessory buying advice with fitment notes and safety checks.
  • Share quick-start checklists for new Tesla owners.

Independence and transparency

TeslaHelper is independently developed and not affiliated with Tesla, Inc. We may earn a commission from select accessory links, but our editorial guidance is based on owner experience and compatibility reviews.

How to reach us

For support or feedback, use the contact page and we will respond as quickly as possible.
