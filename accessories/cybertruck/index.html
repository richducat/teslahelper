Cybertruck accessories guide

Cybertruck accessories need to handle rugged use, weather exposure, and the unique vault storage layout. This guide highlights durable upgrades that protect the stainless exterior and keep the cabin functional.

Rugged essentials

  • Bed and vault organization: modular bins that fit the deep storage area.
  • All-weather liners: heavy-duty mats with tall sidewalls.
  • Screen protection: reduce glare in bright outdoor environments.
  • Charging cable storage: weather-resistant bag or hard case.

Fitment and safety checks

  • Confirm accessories do not block vault drain channels.
  • Use non-abrasive materials against stainless steel surfaces.
  • Keep tie-down points accessible for cargo straps.

Who it is for

Off-road and worksite owners should prioritize vault storage and durable floor protection. Daily drivers may prefer cabin comfort upgrades and an easy-to-clean interior kit.
