Model 3 accessories guide (2024+ Highland and legacy models)

This guide focuses on daily-use upgrades that protect the interior, reduce glare, and keep charging tidy. We separate Highland (2024+) fitment notes from legacy Model 3 (2017–2023) to avoid compatibility issues.

Top priorities for Model 3 owners

  • All-weather floor mats: prioritize full coverage and lip height for snow/sand.
  • Screen protection: matte protectors reduce glare from the larger, brighter display.
  • Console organizers: Highland consoles have deeper bins—measure before buying.
  • Charging kit: include J1772 adapters and cable management for home charging.

How we compare options

  • Fitment accuracy per model year and trim.
  • Material durability (especially in hot climates).
  • Ease of installation and removal for cleaning.
  • Value versus OEM accessories and premium brands.

Recommended accessory mix

We keep a mix of practical and comfort-focused upgrades. Typical picks include floor mats, a tempered glass screen protector, center console trays, a MagSafe phone mount that avoids blocking vents, and brighter trunk LEDs for night loading.

Installation and safety notes

  • Do not route charging cables across pedals or door seals.
  • Use alcohol wipes before applying screen protectors to prevent bubbles.
  • Confirm accessories do not interfere with seat rails or airbag sensors.
