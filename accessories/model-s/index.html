Model S accessories guide (2021+ refresh and legacy trims)

Model S accessories should preserve the premium feel of the cabin while adding practical protection. We focus on quiet, low-profile upgrades that match the larger displays and wider console of the refresh models.

Priority upgrades

  • Dual-screen protection: reduce glare and fingerprints on both displays.
  • Premium floor liners: choose stitched edges to keep the luxury look.
  • Console organizers: separate charging cables from key cards.
  • Trunk storage bins: keep detailing supplies from sliding.

Comparison factors

  • Noise and vibration reduction at highway speed.
  • Compatibility with the rear screen for refresh models.
  • Material quality that matches the dashboard trim.
  • Ease of removing accessories for detailing.

Who it is for

If you value a luxury interior, prioritize screen and console protection first. Performance drivers should add a compact tire inflator and charging adapters for long-distance travel.
