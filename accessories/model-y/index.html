Model Y accessories guide (2020–2025+)

The Model Y is a family and adventure vehicle, so our top picks focus on protecting the cabin, keeping the cargo area organized, and making frequent charging easier. We highlight differences between 2020–2024 and 2025+ refresh trims.

Best-value upgrades

  • All-weather cargo and seat-back protection: essential for pets and sports gear.
  • Rear seat protectors: guard the fold-down hinges and trim on road trips.
  • Matte screen protector: reduces glare in the taller cabin.
  • Center console organizers: added storage for family accessories.

Our testing approach

  • Check second-row clearance and trunk cover compatibility.
  • Test cargo mats with the seats folded flat.
  • Verify charging accessories fit the deeper Model Y center console.
  • Confirm accessories do not block rear HVAC vents.

Who it is for

If you road trip, camp, or haul sports gear, focus on cargo liners and storage solutions first. Urban drivers may prefer a compact phone mount and quick-clean interior mats.
