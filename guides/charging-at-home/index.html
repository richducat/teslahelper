Charging at home: a practical setup guide

Home charging is the most reliable and cost-effective way to keep your Tesla ready. This guide breaks down the setup steps, what equipment you actually need, and how to keep costs predictable.

Step 1: Decide on your charging speed

  • Standard outlet (120V): slow but fine for low-mileage daily driving.
  • 240V outlet: the sweet spot for most owners.
  • Wall Connector: fastest home option with scheduling and load sharing.

Step 2: Plan the install

  • Measure the distance from the panel to your parking spot.
  • Ask an electrician about spare panel capacity.
  • Check local rebates or utility off-peak plans.

Safety and daily habits

Keep cables off walkways, inspect plugs for heat, and set your charge limit based on your daily mileage. For long trips, schedule charging to finish near departure for a warm battery.
