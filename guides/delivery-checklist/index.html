Delivery day checklist for Tesla owners

This checklist keeps delivery day quick and stress-free. Capture issues before you leave the lot and complete the core setup steps so your first drive is smooth.

Exterior inspection

  • Walk the car in natural light and check for paint defects.
  • Inspect glass, lights, and trim alignment.
  • Confirm charge port door opens and closes cleanly.

Interior inspection

  • Check seats, stitching, and headliner for marks.
  • Test the touchscreen and steering wheel controls.
  • Verify both key cards and emergency release tools.

First-day setup

Pair your phone key, set a charge limit, and enable Sentry Mode. Review your software version and connect to Wi-Fi at home for faster updates.
