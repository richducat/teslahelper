FSD (Supervised) explained

FSD (Supervised) is a driver assistance feature that requires active supervision. This guide summarizes the key expectations so you know when to intervene and how to stay compliant with Tesla guidance.

What it can do

  • Navigate on highways with lane changes and exits.
  • Assist with city-street turns and traffic controls.
  • Handle stop-and-go traffic with supervision.

What you must do

  • Keep hands on the wheel and eyes on the road.
  • Be ready to take over instantly at any time.
  • Follow all local traffic laws and signage.

Safety reminders

Tesla regularly updates FSD behavior. Review release notes after each update and avoid using the feature in hazardous conditions such as heavy snow or limited visibility.
