Supercharging basics for Tesla owners

Superchargers are built for fast road-trip charging. The fastest sessions come from warm batteries, shared stalls, and short top-ups instead of full charges.

Before you arrive

  • Use in-car navigation so the battery preconditions.
  • Aim to arrive at 10–20% state of charge for best speeds.
  • Check station status in the Tesla app for availability.

Charging strategy

  • Charge only to what you need to reach the next stop.
  • Move promptly when charging is complete to avoid idle fees.
  • Choose stalls that are not paired when possible.

Cost and etiquette

Rates vary by location and time of day. Follow posted guidance, keep cables tidy, and avoid using charging stalls as parking spaces.
