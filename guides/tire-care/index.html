Tire care for Tesla owners

EVs are heavier and deliver instant torque, which means tires wear faster. Use this guide to keep handling predictable and maintain range.

Monthly checks

  • Check tire pressure monthly and before long trips.
  • Inspect for uneven wear on the inner edge.
  • Verify tread depth before winter or rainy seasons.

Rotation and alignment

  • Rotate every 6,000–8,000 miles or sooner if needed.
  • Get an alignment if the steering wheel is off-center.
  • Use EV-rated tires to reduce road noise and improve efficiency.

Efficiency tips

Slightly lower speeds and maintaining correct pressure can save energy. Combine this with smooth acceleration to extend tire life.
