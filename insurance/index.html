EV insurance tips for Tesla owners

Insurance needs vary by region, mileage, and driving profile. This overview helps you compare coverage and prepare the right questions before you get quotes.

Coverage items to review

  • Battery and OEM parts coverage.
  • Glass coverage for large windshields.
  • Roadside assistance and towing distance limits.

How to compare quotes

Ask for identical coverage limits across providers so you can compare fairly. Note any discounts for safe driving scores or multi-vehicle policies.
